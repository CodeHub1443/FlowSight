## Decisions and deviations (please read)

1. **B4 "proportional to `rateCpm`" and "equals the item travel speed" cannot both hold with items at a constant 0.9 m/s.** Items now travel at `beltSpeedFor(rate)`:
   proportional up to `BELT_FULL_CPM = 6` cartons a minute, then 0.9 m/s, with a floor of `BELT_MIN_FRACTION = 0.25` while the belt moves (so run-off items and the
   first cartons of a visit do not stall). The scroll uses the same function, so a unit test compares them rate for rate (0.5 to 40 cpm) and compares the constants. Strictly
   proportional below 6 cpm only, and the floor means a tiny non-zero rate still scrolls at a quarter speed. Mock rates are around 14 cpm (docs/03 example 14.2), so in practice
   items run at the old 0.9 m/s. Both numbers are in `beltItems.ts`; owner call if the calibration should differ.
2. **Pill separator is ` · ` (docs/04 section 1), not the ASCII ` - ` of the brief and the t22 spec.** One constant, `PILL_SEP` in `labels/actorLabel.ts`.
3. **X-ray has no control.** `uiStore.xray` exists and the glyphs follow it, but nothing in the app sets it (no button in `MapControls`, searched `src/app` and `src/features`).
   Until a control exists the glyphs show in practice only for cameras that are not `OK`. The control belongs in a shared file (`MapControls`), so it is not added here. For
   tests, debug builds expose `window.__uiStore` (LiveScene).
4. **Gap in a shared file: `src/stores/accessors.ts` has no health accessor.** The brief says "read through the existing accessors"; there is none for `HealthFrame`. LiveScene
   reads `liveStore.getState().health?.cams` and subscribes with the same pattern as `subscribeBay`. Suggest `getCams`/`subscribeCams` in `accessors.ts` in a later shared change;
   LiveScene's feed would then call them.
5. **Actor ids needed no shared-file change.** Truck = `visitId` (`resolveVisit`), person and forklift = track id (`findActor`), as `TruckBody`, `PersonBody` and `ForkliftBody`
   expect. Existing quirk, unchanged: track ids repeat across bays in fixture data and `findActor` takes the first bay (the scene's `ActorTracks` is keyed by id alone as well).
6. **An actor in the scene replaces the bay marker.** LiveScene passes `selectedBayId = null` when the selected truck, person or forklift exists in the live data, so the
   bay brackets and the bay pill do not sit on top of the actor's marker (a person at the belt mouth would put the two pills on one spot). A selection the scene cannot find
   still marks its bay, as before. Behaviour change for tray clicks on trucks.
7. **The shutter hood is always drawn.** Before, only the open state drew it, and the live scene was always open, so the closed look was never on screen. Rolled-up curtain and hood now match in both states.
8. **Boom sections are longer than before** (3.59 m each, so they stay joined at full run-out). At rest their extra length is inside the housing, below the belt and inside the
   side beams; obstacle footprints keep the old lengths (`conveyorBodyItems` is unchanged for them). This is the item most worth a close look in 22B.2.
9. **Contact shadows**: boom and shutter are static scenery baked into the contact-shadow texture, so `contactShadows.invalidate()` fires once when a bay's boom or shutter comes to rest.
   New floor rings and glyphs are on the actor layer so they are never baked in.
10. **Guard test updated**: `noFrameState.test.ts` lists every `useFrame` user with a reason; the four new ones are added (ActorInteraction, Bay, CameraGlyphs, DynamicBoxes).
11. **Debug hooks (debug builds only, like `__actorProbe`)**: `window.__pickProbe` (screen position of every pickable bound, hover look counters) and `window.__uiStore`. The e2e needs them because nothing on a canvas can be queried.
12. Not done, by design: no new assets, lighting, palette, camera preset, quality tier or apron change. No forklift LOD.

## Draw calls and GPU resources

- New GPU resources: floor-ring materials (2) and the 21-glyph `InstancedMesh` geometry, all per mount and disposed in effect cleanups; ring geometries come from the shared `roundedRing` cache
  (already registered with `gpuRelease`); `DynamicBoxes` geometries go through `useInstanceGeometry` like `BoxInstances`. No textures, no module-level resources. 22B.2 must confirm the nav-leak counts are flat.
- Draw calls (estimate, not measured): the boom is now two meshes (hi, lo), the ticks one, the shutter two (slab, ribs), the tip roller one; the old bodies and doors were three each. About +4 per bay, +1 for the glyphs,
  and two hidden marker groups: roughly +30 on Med before the shadow pass. 22B.2 should read `renderer.info` before and after.
